Safe sharing rules

1. Verify recipient separately using a known contact method.
2. Preview the exact item; avoid sending whole albums.
3. Pause if the request is urgent or unexpected.
4. Re-check recipient inside the trusted sharing app.

Payment safety checklist

Use before approving a UPI collect request, QR payment, link or voice-assistant payment.

Did I initiate this payment myself?
Did I verify the person/business and amount independently?
Do I understand that entering a UPI PIN usually authorizes a debit/payment?
Did I reject requests for OTP, PIN, CVV or remote access?
Did I review the final amount and payee in the official app?
Complete every check before proceeding.

Voice-assistant guardrails

Do not let a voice command alone authorize money movement. Verify recipient and amount on the official payment app and use authentication where available.
  • Disable voice purchasing/payment features you do not use.
  • Never share OTPs or PINs with a person, bot or assistant.
  • Set transaction limits and bank alerts if available.
  • Review permissions and remove unknown apps.
  • In India, report financial cyber fraud quickly via 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

PRIVA cannot connect to Google Pay or block payments from this website.

Personal security checkup

Tick only steps you have actually completed. Progress is session-only.

Use a unique phone screen lock; hide sensitive lock-screen previews.
Enable 2-step verification on primary email/Google account.
Review app permissions; remove unnecessary access.
Install OS and app updates from official sources.
Set up official lost-device recovery and account recovery.
Never share OTP, PIN or recovery codes.

If you suspect compromise

  1. From a trusted device, change the affected password and sign out unknown sessions.
  2. Contact your bank immediately if a payment may be affected.
  3. Review recently installed apps and powerful permissions (Accessibility, Device admin, VPN).
  4. Keep transaction IDs, messages and URLs as evidence.
  5. In India, report cyber financial fraud via 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.

Avoid random “spyware remover” APKs or remote access from unknown callers.

What it can do

  • Encrypt manually selected files for download.
  • Decrypt PRIVA files with the correct passphrase.
  • Check pasted text for known scam signals.
  • Guide safe sharing/payment decisions.
  • Provide a security checklist and report.

What it cannot do

  • Protect the entire gallery or other apps automatically.
  • Access Google Pay private data or block payments.
  • Detect every hacker, spyware app, leak or foreign login.
  • Send/recall files in WhatsApp or another app.
  • Guarantee “100% security.”
Important: This is an educational MVP, not security-audited software. Do not use it as the only protection for highly sensitive files. Keep encrypted files and passphrases safe. A compromised device can capture data before encryption or after decryption.

Privacy design

No backend, analytics or network-upload code is included. Files are handled by your browser; encrypted output is downloaded. Host on trusted HTTPS, avoid third-party scripts, and use an updated device.

PRIVA • Hackathon MVP • Local-first by design • Not a substitute for OS, bank or account security controls.
